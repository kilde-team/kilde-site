kilde records screens, system audio and microphones. Whether a given recording is lawful depends on where you are, who else is present, and what is being recorded — and it is entirely your responsibility to determine that before you press record.

Depending on your jurisdiction, this may include:

  • Consent. Some places require every participant in a conversation to consent to it being recorded; others require only one. Meeting platforms and employers frequently impose their own notice requirements on top of the law.
  • Workplace and platform rules. Your employer, your client, your school, or the service hosting a meeting may prohibit or restrict recording regardless of what the law allows.
  • Copyright and licensing. Capturing streamed video, music, paid courses or other protected material may be restricted by copyright law or by the service's terms.
  • Confidentiality and data protection. A recording can contain other people's personal data, and handling it may bring obligations under data protection law or a confidentiality agreement.

kilde provides the capability; it does not and cannot verify that you are entitled to use it in a particular situation. If you are unsure, seek your own legal advice.
