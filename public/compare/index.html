How kilde compares

Prices and features confirmed September 28, 2026

If you arrived here looking for an Audio Hijack alternative, a Granola alternative, or because the screen recorder in QuickTime Player would not capture the sound your Mac plays — this page lines kilde up against those tools. kilde records a meeting the way it happens: the screen together with both sides of the conversation, in one file, transcribed on your Mac. Every claim below links to the vendor's own page, with the date we confirmed it. These are the highlights that matter for meeting recording, not exhaustive feature lists, and other tools may fit other jobs better.

Which tool fits which job

QuickTime Player

Right for a quick, silent screencast — it is already installed and costs nothing. When the Mac's own sound needs to be in the file, it has no path to it.

OBS Studio

A broad studio for live streaming and scene compositing. Powerful, and heavier than a meeting needs; per-application audio capture on macOS arrived with OBS 30.

Audio Hijack

Audio-first production: per-app capture, effects and recording chains for podcasts. A fine choice when the screen is not part of what you are keeping.

Granola

Meeting notes synced through the cloud, on every device — when the notes matter more than the recording itself. Your transcripts and notes live in its cloud, not on your Mac.

kilde

Meetings: the screen with both sides of the conversation in one file, transcribed on your Mac. No bot joins the call, nothing is uploaded, and no virtual audio driver is installed.
